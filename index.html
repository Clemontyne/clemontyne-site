LEAD CAPTURE & FOLLOW-UP

When leads fall through the cracks

No real system to capture a lead, follow up, and move them through a funnel — so people quietly disappear before they ever become a client. We see this constantly with coaches, consultants, and instructors running everything through email and spreadsheets.

DAY-TO-DAY OPERATIONS

Running on memory instead of a system

Attendance, billing, and communication tracked by memory, paper, or a spreadsheet only one person understands — so nothing's connected and everything depends on someone remembering. Common for youth programs and membership-based businesses.

PIPELINE & CLIENT TRACKING

Deals and clients living in inboxes

No real pipeline — just inboxes, sticky notes, and whoever remembers to follow up. Deals stall and client details get lost between people. Common for small agencies and local B2B service businesses.

WEBSITE & VISIBILITY

No front door for your business

An outdated site, one that's hard to find, or no website at all — so people searching for what you do land nowhere useful and move on to whoever shows up next. Common across almost every type of business that's grown on referrals and never needed a website until now.

QUESTIONS

Before you reach out

Do we own the code after the build?

Yes — you own your specific system: your data, your branding, your customizations. We retain rights to the underlying template architecture we build from, since that's what lets us deliver custom work at a fair price instead of starting from a blank page every time.

How long does a build actually take?

Depends on scope. A Starter CRM typically takes 1–2 weeks, a full Website + Intake + CRM package runs 2–4 weeks, and a complete three-layer Operating System takes 4–6 weeks. You'll get a specific timeline before anything starts.

What happens after launch?

The Full Operating System includes 30 days of post-launch support for anything that comes up. After that — or for other packages — ongoing help is available through a monthly support retainer, starting at $300/month.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds are included in every package. Additional rounds bill at our standard hourly rate, laid out clearly in your proposal before we start — no surprises partway through.

Do you build on Wix, Squarespace, or other no-code platforms?

No — everything we build is custom-coded from the ground up. It costs a little more upfront than a template builder, but you're not boxed in by what a platform allows, and the system is actually yours.

Is the site itself accessible?

We build with accessibility in mind from the start — proper structure, alt text, keyboard navigation — rather than bolting it on after the fact.

What if we need something added after launch?

That's a Template Customization Sprint — a focused, scoped addition sized as its own small project rather than open-ended scope creep on the original build.
