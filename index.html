Automation, plainly explained

We build three levels of automation into every system.

Type 1

Trigger & Notify

Something happens, someone gets told. A new lead comes in, you get pinged — no more checking five places to see what's new.

Type 2

Sequence & Follow-up

A chain of steps runs on its own — reminders, follow-ups, and check-ins that happen on schedule, without anyone needing to remember.

Type 3

Calculated & Conditional

The system makes a decision based on your rules — flagging overdue payments, routing leads, or surfacing what needs attention first.
